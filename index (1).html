BEFORE WE GET STARTED

A few things
you might be wondering.

What do you need for a quote?

Start with your project type, city, approximate dimensions and material preference. Photos, a rough sketch or cabinet plans help. Exact measurements and the final scope need confirmation before fabrication.

Can you help me choose a material?

Select “Help me choose” on the form. Share how you use the space, your colors and any inspiration you have.

Do you serve my area?

Include your city or ZIP so the team can confirm coverage and travel requirements. The company website lists 802 Butte Ave SE in Pacific, Washington. Please call before visiting.

How long does a project take?

Timing depends on material availability, measurements, scope and scheduling. Share your target date so the team can confirm availability for your project.

Can I verify registration and insurance?

The public HomeAdvisor listing shows registration DESIGNS814R6. Check current status with Washington L&I and request current insurance documentation directly from the team.
